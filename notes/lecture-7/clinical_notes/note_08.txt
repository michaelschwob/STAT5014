Sample Name: Consult - Dyspnea
Medical Specialty: Cardiovascular / Pulmonary

SUBJECTIVE: The patient reports dyspnea on exertion that has worsened over three months.

PHYSICAL EXAMINATION
VITAL SIGNS: BP 130-140/80-90 on repeat measurement, pulse 84.
LUNGS: Decreased breath sounds at the bases.

IMPRESSION:
1. Dyspnea on exertion, etiology unclear.
2. Essential hypertension.
