Sample Name: SOAP - Shortness of Breath
Medical Specialty: Cardiovascular / Pulmonary

SUBJECTIVE: The patient is admitted for shortness of breath, continues to do fairly well. The patient has chronic atrial fibrillation, on anticoagulation, INR of 1.72.

PHYSICAL EXAMINATION
VITAL SIGNS: Pulse of 78 and blood pressure 130/60.
LUNGS: Clear.
HEART: A soft systolic murmur in the aortic area.

IMPRESSION:
1. Status shortness of breath responding well to medical management.
2. Atrial fibrillation, chronic, on anticoagulation.
3. Aortic stenosis.
